Import Bathrooms from CSV

Each row needs coordinates (latitude and longitude, or a point column in WKT POINT (lon lat)) or a geocodable street address with zip or zip_code. If resource_type is present, only restroom rows are imported. public_access_hours_*, public_access_days, notes, park, source, and access are mapped into hours and remarks.

NYC-style columns are supported too: facility name, hours of operation, location (POINT WKT), accessibility, restroom type, changing stations, additional notes, and website.

Missing zips are filled from another row with the same park or analysis_neighborhood, then by reverse-geocoding. UTF-8, Windows-1252, and Latin-1 encodings are supported.
